Scribo lives here ...

Scribo is a free to play word game that challenges your vocabulary and spelling skills. ' 'Click the link above to play Scribo online

Simply move the tiles from the picker on the left on the board in the middle. When you make a word it is indicated on the board, and added to the words you have discovered.

You might have noted each tile has a value as well as a letter. As you make words the value of the word is added to your scores. You score for all the words you find, ones on the board, and a board completion score.
